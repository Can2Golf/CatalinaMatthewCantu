OURLOVESTORY

  1. 2021

    Winter 2021 · Austin, TX

    The Meeting

    Matthew and Catalina met in 2021 in the most scary way possible — an online poetry class during COVID. Matthew was on campus at Our Lady of the Lake University for golf, while Catalina was taking classes remotely from home in Austin. During the icy winter storm, Catalina messaged Matthew for help with their poetry homework.

    At the time, they only knew each other through Instagram, but that one message turned into something neither of them expected. When classes returned to campus in August, Catalina and Matthew finally met in person.

  2. 2022

    2022 · Falling in Love

    Finding Love

    What was supposed to be a simple hangout turned into them staying up until 2 a.m., just talking and getting to know each other. After countless questions, laughs, and late-night conversations, Catalina had a feeling Matthew was someone special. And when she opened her apartment door and saw him for the first time, she realized he truly checked all the boxes — a detail you can't exactly tell over Instagram: dark, tall, handsome, with a calm confidence that made everything feel easy.

    After six months of being inseparable (and Catalina's persistence despite Matthew's insistence that he “wasn't looking for a relationship”), Catalina finally gave him an ultimatum: “I can't be your friend anymore unless we're together.” Not long after, Matthew asked her to be his girlfriend.

  3. March 1, 2026

    Central Park · New York City

    The Proposal

    Three years later, on March 1, 2026, Matthew proposed in Central Park in New York City. As if the moment wasn't magical enough, it started snowing at the perfect time, turning their proposal into something straight out of a movie and a memory they'll never forget.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What time should I arrive?

We recommend getting to the church at least 30 minutes before 2 PM so you have time to settle in before the ceremony begins. Mass will begin right at 2 PM.

Can I bring a +1?

Access is a small and intimate gathering; we kindly ask that only close family and friends attend unless stated otherwise. Please refer to your invitation for details.

Are kids allowed?

While we absolutely adore your little ones, we're kindly keeping our wedding celebration (ceremony and reception) an adults-only evening to maintain a calm and comfortable atmosphere during the ceremony and reception.

Where should I park?

There is paid parking near the venue and a few free street-parking spots available. In the first approaches, we'll send the exact location and parking details via SMS. PWD-friendly parking is available. If you need assistance or special arrangements, please don't hesitate to let us know.
